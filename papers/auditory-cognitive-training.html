Personalized auditory-cognitive training

A randomized controlled study of naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in older adults with hearing loss.

Ockelmann, J. F., Stropahl, M., Scherpiet, S., & Giroud, N. R. · npj Science of Learning, 10(1), 80.

Abstract

This randomized controlled study examined whether four weeks of personalized, gamified auditory-cognitive training affected naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in older adults with mild-to-moderate hearing loss. Participants in the training group showed improvements in comprehension that were maintained at follow-up, alongside changes in some cognitive measures. The active control group showed no comparable change in comprehension.
