Personalized auditory-cognitive training

Effects of personalized, gamified training on naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in older adults with hearing loss.

Ockelmann, J. F., Stropahl, M., Scherpiet, S., & Giroud, N. R. · npj Science of Learning, 10(1), 80.

Abstract

This randomized controlled study tested whether four weeks of personalized, gamified auditory-cognitive training could improve naturalistic speech-in-noise comprehension in older adults with mild-to-moderate hearing loss. Participants completing the training showed significant and sustained improvements in comprehension, alongside partial cognitive gains, while an active control group showed no comparable benefit. The findings support auditory-cognitive training as a potential complement to conventional hearing rehabilitation.
