Cognitive differences moderate alpha power association with listening effort and accuracy

Associations between cognitive capacity, acoustic context, neural alpha activity, and listening outcomes.

Ockelmann, J. F., Elmer, S., & Giroud, N. · Under review at Scientific Reports · Preprint available.

Abstract

This EEG study examined variation in the relationship between neural alpha activity and speech-in-noise outcomes across listeners and acoustic conditions. In older adults with hearing loss, alpha modulation changed non-linearly with noise level. Its associations with comprehension and listening effort also varied with working memory, selective attention, and acoustic context.
