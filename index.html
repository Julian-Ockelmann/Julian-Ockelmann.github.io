Profile

I hold a PhD in Cognitive Neuroscience from the University of Zurich and currently work as a 60% postdoctoral researcher in Silvia Brem's lab. My current work includes GraphoLearn EEG analysis and the development of open-access, distributable, replicable research tasks in Python.
